ResLife Guest Experience

Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ResLife Guest Experience, the ResLife Church Guest Experience volunteer team in Grandville, MI, collects and uses information for our captain text alert program.

What we collect

When a team captain signs up for text alerts, we collect their name, the team they lead, and their mobile phone number. When a volunteer uses the Guest Experience Hub to tell their captain they can't serve, we receive the volunteer's name and the date they can't serve so we can pass it to that captain.

How we use it

We use this information only to send captains text messages about volunteer availability for their team, to answer HELP requests, and to honor STOP requests.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Phone numbers are provided only to the service providers needed to deliver our messages (such as our SMS provider, Twilio), and only for that purpose. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Keeping and removing your information

We keep a captain's information while they serve as a captain. Reply STOP at any time to stop messages, or email reslifecaptains@gmail.com to have your information removed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: reslifecaptains@gmail.com.
